# cheap-flights-app

Flight comparison site for Central European travelers. Compares prices from 5 departure airports: Bratislava (BTS), Vienna (VIE), Budapest (BUD), Košice (KSC), Prague (PRG).
Solo project by Lukas. Monetization: affiliate links via Travelpayouts / Aviasales.

Live: https://cheap-flights-app.vercel.app
Repo: github.com/Haldo1231/cheap-flights-app

- Travelpayouts Data API (free tier, cache-based)

- THE PRICE STORE (how every price reaches a screen): `app/lib/tpV3.ts` = the Travelpayouts v3 "prices for dates" method (month summaries with `unique=true` = every destination of an airport with its cheapest ticket; route-months = one ticket per day, or per departure+return day; real tickets with airline, flight number, time, booking site, deep link; no `found_at` in the data, so `found_at` = when we fetched it; the month guard and one-way/round-trip checks are in `normalizeV3`). `app/lib/priceStore.ts` = reads/writes (upsert key origin+destination+departure_date+return_date+one_way; Supabase answers at most 1000 rows, reads page). `app/lib/priceSource.ts` = STORE FIRST, LIVE FALLBACK: `routePairTickets` / `routeMonthTickets` / `overviewTickets` read the store when fresh (`FRESH_HOURS` = 48), else fetch live, store and `console.warn('[prices] live fallback …')`; live fails → stored rows with their real age (`console.error`); store unusable → the live answer directly; route-months are remembered in-process for a minute. `/api/cron/refresh-prices` (vercel.json 02:00 UTC, Bearer `CRON_SECRET`) = the nightly job: summaries for the 5 airports × this month + 3 (`coveredMonths`), then for the 12 named + 25 cheapest destinations of each summary all three kinds (rt out, ow out, ow back); time-boxed slices (`PRICE_REFRESH_BUDGET_MS`, default 180 s, capped by `sliceBudget` so the hand-over fits in `maxDuration`) that call themselves (`?run=ID`, `PRICE_REFRESH_SELF_URL` / `VERCEL_PROJECT_PRODUCTION_URL`): every call answers 202 first and works in `after()`, the caller waits for that 202 and tries the hand-over 3 times (`startSlice`, `app/lib/priceRun.ts`); a run with no progress for 30 min (`STUCK_AFTER_MS`, newest `done_at`) is resumed by the daily deals cron (`resumeStuckRun`, the watchdog) or by the next nightly call, older than 20 h it is given up; failed jobs get up to 5 tries in total (`requeueFailedJobs`); paced ≤ ~190 requests/min, log in `price_runs` / `price_jobs`, departed days deleted; the 40 summary jobs are planned MONTH FIRST with the airport order rotated per month (`summaryJobs`), which is also the order the route jobs get, so a night that stops half way leaves the far months stale instead of starving whole airports. `/api/debug-prices?key=…&store=1` = store status. The token travels in the `X-Access-Token` header (`app/lib/travelpayouts.ts`, `tpFetch`), never in an address.

## Known limitations (not bugs)
- Travelpayouts free data is cached: small routes (e.g. KSC → Prague) can return no results.
- Prices can be stale, so always show them as "from €X".
- Some cached fares have no airline or departure time. Show "Airline not in data" instead of guessing. Never invent flight data.
- The price store is refreshed once a night; a route-month a visitor asks for that is not in it (or older than 48 h) costs a live request per airport and kind, then it is in the store. If a live answer is 429 (too many requests), the stored rows (if any) are shown and the failure is logged.
- The nightly job's self-chaining, HTTP 508: from 18 to 24 Nov 2026 EVERY run stopped after exactly 9 slices (runs 5-11 in `price_runs`): the call from one slice to the next was answered `HTTP 508` three tries in a row, and ~1,100 of ~2,250 route jobs never ran. 508 is Vercel's `INFINITE_LOOP_DETECTED` ("Loop Detected"), documented as happening when an application makes requests to itself — which is exactly what a slice does. Vercel documents no number for when it starts; what we saw is that roughly the first 8 self-calls of a chain go through and then 508 stays (the 06:00 watchdog got one more slice and its hand-over was refused at once). Runs 1-4 (15-17 Nov) still finished with 16-23 slices, so something changed on Vercel's side; the exact rule is unknown. The fix is to need far fewer self-calls: a slice works 180 s instead of 50 s (`DEFAULT_BUDGET_MS`), so a night is ~5 slices. ## Decisions
- Route pages read the price store ONLY, never the live price service: 47 pages x 4 months x 2 kinds would be hundreds of requests on every rebuild, and every price carries its real age instead (24 Nov 2026).
- `data/routes.json` is committed rather than built at render time, so a route page's address can never appear or disappear between deployments (24 Nov 2026).
- The flight time on a route page comes only from ONE-WAY no-stop tickets: for a round-trip ticket the data does not say whether `duration` covers one leg or both, and a wrong flight time is worse than none (24 Nov 2026).
- The "cheapest day of the week" ranking uses one-way fares only: a round-trip price belongs to a pair of days, so it says nothing about a single weekday (24 Nov 2026).
- A route page's structured data is BreadcrumbList only. There is no search-engine result type for a flight price, and marking cached fares up as firm offers would claim more than the data says (24 Nov 2026).
- The nightly price job's slice is 180 s instead of 50 s, because Vercel refuses a chain's later self-calls with HTTP 508 (24 Nov 2026).
- The nightly job plans its 40 summary jobs month by month, with the five airports rotated one step per month, instead of airport by airport: the job order is what a night that stops early throws away, and the old order threw away the same two airports (Košice, Prague) every time (24 Nov 2026).
- Guides may be written from OUR OWN stored prices and cost numbers; anything that would have to be looked up outside is left out rather than guessed. Published coach and bus fares, and even a Prague airport bus number, differ between sources and change, so the guides name operators and give times in round terms and print no outside fares and no outside links (one guessed official link was already a 404) (24 Nov 2026).
- A guide written here is `draft: true` and stays unpublished until the owner has read it and says to publish: the text goes out under his name, so publishing is his call, not ours. The first three were approved and published on 24 Nov 2026. Trip reports stay his alone (24 Nov 2026).
- A table in an article scrolls sideways instead of squeezing: `.article` sets `overflow-wrap: anywhere` for long links, which inside a narrow table cell split city names and prices in half on a phone (24 Nov 2026).
- Next.js is kept on the security updates of its own major (16.4.2 → 16.4.6, 24 Nov 2026) and nothing more: `experimental.agentUpgrade` is set to `security` in `next.config.ts`, so `next upgrade` offers the patch that closes an advisory and never a version that would need a migration. The advisory that prompted it (GHSA-mfr8-8qww-v76h, `use cache` / `unstable_cache`) did not affect this site, but the notice stopped every agent-run build until it was taken.
- robots.txt blocks only `/api/`: `/results`, `/trip` and `/back` carry noindex, and a blocked page's noindex is never seen, so Google could list the bare address (24 Nov 2026).
- `pageMetadata` drops " — cheapflights" when title + site name would pass 60 characters, so Google does not cut the meaningful words (24 Nov 2026).
- A saved route keeps the ROUTE AND DATES, not a ticket: cached tickets disappear or change price within days, so a saved ticket would soon be a dead link, while a route + dates can be priced again every day from the same store as the search. The only price copied is the one number at save time, for the "then → now" line (24 Nov 2026).
- The heart on the destination and route pages saves date mode `any` (no date picked = the cheapest round trip in the covered months), because those pages show prices with no date chosen (24 Nov 2026).
- Sign-in is Google only: Supabase's free shared e-mail sender allows only a few mails per hour, too few for magic links or password mails (24 Nov 2026).
