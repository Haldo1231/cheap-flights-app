A cheap flight is not a cheap trip. A €26 return to Milan and a €32 return to Krakow look like the same deal until you
add four nights of sleeping and eating, and then one of them costs half as much as the other.

So this site estimates the whole trip, not just the flight. This guide shows the numbers behind that estimate, because
an estimate you cannot inspect is not worth much.

# What one day costs, city by city

These are the daily figures the site uses. Each one is a bed in a hostel, food, local transport and small extras, per
person, per day:

| City | Bed | Food | Transport | Extras | One day |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Krakow | €14 | €12 | €4 | €6 | **€36** |
| Warsaw | €15 | €12 | €4 | €6 | **€37** |
| Athens | €18 | €15 | €5 | €8 | **€46** |
| Lisbon | €20 | €16 | €5 | €8 | **€49** |
| Madrid | €22 | €18 | €6 | €10 | **€56** |
| Barcelona | €22 | €18 | €6 | €10 | **€56** |
| Berlin | €24 | €18 | €7 | €10 | **€59** |
| Rome | €25 | €20 | €8 | €10 | **€63** |
| Milan | €28 | €22 | €7 | €10 | **€67** |
| Amsterdam | €30 | €22 | €6 | €10 | **€68** |
| Paris | €30 | €25 | €9 | €12 | **€76** |
| London | €32 | €25 | €10 | €12 | **€79** |

As of 24 November 2026. The live figure for each city is on its own page, which is the number to trust if these ever
drift apart.

The spread is the useful part: a day in London costs more than twice a day in Krakow. Over a four-day trip that is a
difference of about €170 per person, which is more than most of the flights on this site.

# The three spending styles

The table above is the middle setting. The site also offers two others, and they are simply multipliers on the same
four lines:

- **Ultra cheap** is a dorm bed, street food and groceries, walking or a day pass, and mostly free sights. It cuts
  food to 60% of the middle figure, transport to 50% and extras to 30%, and takes 20% off the bed.
- **Middle** is the table above: a hostel bed, cafés and simple restaurants, public transport, a few paid sights.
- **Rich** is a private room or hotel, restaurants every day, taxis and paid sights. It triples the bed, transport and
  extras, and multiplies food by 2.5.

Those multipliers are our assumptions, not measurements. They live in one file in the code so they can be corrected
in one place, and every screen uses the same ones, which is why no two pages on this site ever disagree about a total.

# How a trip total is worked out

    total = flight + (days of stay × cost of one day)

The flight is the whole fare: for a return trip that is both flights together, because a return fare is one price for
two flights and splitting it per leg would be made up. Days of stay are the nights between your two dates, with a
minimum of one.

Worked through for three nights in Krakow with a €40 return: €40 + (3 × €36) = **€148**. The same three nights in
Paris with the same €40 flight: €40 + (3 × €76) = **€268**.

# What the estimate leaves out

Being clear about this matters more than the numbers themselves:

- **Bags.** Low-cost fares on these routes are usually hand luggage only, and a hold bag is extra. Check what your
  fare includes before you compare it with another.
- **Getting to your departure airport.** If you are flying from Vienna or Budapest rather than your nearest airport,
  that coach or train is real money. See [the guide to the five airports](/guides/five-airports-one-search).
- **Big one-off tickets.** A match, a concert or a named museum can cost more than a day of everything else.
- **Travel insurance, and the money you spend because you are on holiday.**

And one rule we keep: when a city is missing any of its four cost numbers, the site says "no cost data" rather than
guessing. A trip to that city is never counted as being inside your budget, because we would not know.

# The flight is usually the smaller half

This is the part most people have backwards. Looking at the cheapest return fares we held to these 12 cities in late
November 2026, the median cheapest return was €148 for a November departure, €130 for December, €104 for January and
€97 for February. Against €36 to €79 a day for being there, the flight stops being the main decision as soon as the
trip is longer than two or three nights.

Which leads to the practical conclusion: if you want a cheaper trip and you have already found a cheap flight, the
next lever is not a cheaper flight. It is a cheaper city, a cheaper bed, or one night fewer.
